# Girt Services LTD — static Concept Demo brief

Status: local static Concept Demo only. Customer authorisation remains **NOT VERIFIED**.

## Audience and first-screen task

Primary audience: a Liverpool-area homeowner or property manager deciding whether to call/book carpet or upholstery cleaning. The first screen must make the business, regional base, principal cleaning focus and usable next step clear in five seconds.

The page does not claim that the customer commissioned it. It must route the primary action to the business’s existing public booking page and never reproduce its calculator, deposit or payment workflow.

## Design read

`Design Read: Liverpool domestic cleaning · precise, grounded home-care · register=brand · SOUL=7 / SPECTACLE=3 / DENSITY=5 · hero-engine=static photographic composition.`

Lazy default rejected: pale green, six identical cleaning cards and generic bubbles. Chosen direction: deep slate, warm timber and one citrus-orange action colour, with tactile cleaning photography and compact, factual content.

## Page hierarchy and commercial tasks

1. Hero: clarify Girt Services, Liverpool, carpet/upholstery focus and external booking route.
2. Service mosaic: make the three core service contexts understandable without a long SEO list.
3. Method: translate the official carpet-cleaning process into a readable four-step reassurance sequence.
4. Proof: point to current independent customer feedback without inventing scores or copying reviews.
5. Bottom 40%: use patio-care concept visual to show the broader surface-care scope, then reconnect the visitor with the public booking/email/telephone path.
6. Close: context-aware Liverpool contact details and the small, truthful `Independent concept preview` label.

## Facts, CTA and non-scope

Facts come only from `../research_20260901/CUSTOMER_PUBLIC_INTELLIGENCE.md` and its source ledger: company name, Liverpool address, telephone, public email, core service names, public booking destination, and third-party current review availability.

Primary CTA: `Book via Girt Services` → `https://girtcarpetcleaning.co.uk/`.

Secondary CTA: public `tel:` and `mailto:` links. Non-scope: no form, payment, booking calculator, customer data collection, claims of turnaround, unsourced guarantees, invented projects, deployments, email or social outreach.

## Implementation note

This is a static HTML/CSS page. Three image assets are original concept visuals, never described as real Girt jobs. The design must remain coherent if these images are removed, and has a reduced-motion safe static treatment because no automatic motion is used.
